Closing this: it is superseded. We took Simon's advice above. The "-TTF"/"-OTF" .sfd and .vfb files are FontForge and FontLab imports of the shipped binaries (quadratic outlines, the binaries' TrueType instructions, a .vfb twin of the same style), so those families now get fresh .glyphs sources converted from the Google Fonts binaries with babelfont's TTF reader. Families with an original source (.vfb or .sfd) are converted from that. Each conversion lives in a googlefonts/<family> repository, and METADATA.pb points at it in its own PR (#11077, #11162, #11218 so far).
