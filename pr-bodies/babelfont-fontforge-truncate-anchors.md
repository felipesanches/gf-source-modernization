FontForge's GPOS export truncates anchor coordinates toward zero, while fontc rounds them, so marks attached through a fractional anchor land a unit away. This opt-in filter truncates anchors the same way. In Thabit-Bold and Thabit-BoldOblique it takes the shaping differences against the FontForge release from about 7000 and 11000 runs to 0.
