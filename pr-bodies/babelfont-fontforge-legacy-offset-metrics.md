Body:

FontForge exporters before 2014-09-16 (ee15007274e9) resolved offset-mode usWinAscent/usWinDescent against a head box built from on-curve points only, and hhea ascender/descender from the true outline extremes truncated toward zero and widened to that box; the reader's default (floor/ceil of the true outline) differs by a unit or two wherever a curve's control point lies beyond its extreme. This opt-in filter re-resolves those four metrics from the stated deltas, which the SFD reader now records as `sfd.offset_delta.<key>`, and marks them absolute so an SFD write-back stays consistent. With it, the converted Mountains of Christmas Bold, Nothing You Could Do and UnifrakturMaguntia get the vertical metrics their Google Fonts releases ship; across 149 converted styles it changes only 4 and moves no metric away from its release. The rule was validated on quadratic sources only, and does not model the old sethhead quirk where the hhea descender's mode came from HheadAOffset.
