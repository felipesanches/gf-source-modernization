Metadata only, no font binaries change. One commit per family: the METADATA.pb `source { }` block and upstream_info.md. Follows #11162.

No source of these families was ever published: the fonts Google Fonts ships were their only form. Each now points at a new repository, https://github.com/googlefonts/<family>, whose history is the shipped fonts imported unmodified, the Unified Font Repository template, and the conversion to `.glyphs` last. METADATA.pb records that conversion commit.

- 16 families, 37 styles: Black And White Picture, Black Han Sans, Cute Font, Dokdo, East Sea Dokdo, Gamja Flower, Gugi, Hi Melody, Lato, Nanum Brush Script, Nanum Gothic, Nanum Pen Script, Poor Story, Song Myung, Stylish, Sunflower.
- These needed two more converter changes than #11162's families: localized names no Glyphs property holds (the Korean names) written as Name Table Entries, and the typographic family and subfamily (name IDs 16/17) kept. Converted with babelfont-rs's TTF reader, no filters: babelfont 7e58ba5, branch 2026-10-23 of https://github.com/felipesanches/babelfont-rs, which is the branch #11162 used plus those two changes, all proposed to simoncozens/babelfont-rs. Built with gftools-builder (gftools-rust 1e638bd, fontc 1.0.0).
- Every build matches the binaries google/fonts ships: 0 blocking rows under the table gate, exactly the shipped codepoints, and functionally equivalent (cmap, shaping, rendering, names, line spacing, advances, GDEF).
- The one known difference, reviewed and kept, is hinting: the builds are hinted by ttfautohint, not by the release's instructions. It is listed in each upstream_info.md and repository README.
- The previous source blocks and the earlier investigations are kept in each upstream_info.md.

Tools, plans and logs: https://github.com/felipesanches/gf-source-modernization at ffc7800.
