Body:

The SFD reader (GlyphClass 4, or a mark inferred from its anchors) and --set-subcategory gave every mark subCategory Nonspacing, and a Glyphs compiler zeroes such a mark's advance, while FontForge exports the width the source states. A mark with a non-zero advance is now Spacing Combining: still a GDEF mark, with its advance kept. Lohit-Tamil u0BC1/u0BC2 (532/776), six Cardo-Italic marks (e.g. uni2E0F 2443) and two Cardo-Regular marks keep the advances FontForge exported.
