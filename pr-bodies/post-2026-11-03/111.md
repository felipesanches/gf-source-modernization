The problem: an SFD glyph has exactly the anchor points it lists, since the format has no anchor inheritance from references. Glyphs, by default, gives a glyph the anchors of the components it is made of, so a composite written to Glyphs gains anchors the SFD does not state. That is an import problem, so the flag and the filter are gone. Instead the model has a field for it, `Font::propagate_anchors` (`None` = the format's default): the SFD reader sets it to `false`, and the Glyphs reader and writer map it to and from the "Propagate Anchors" custom parameter, so it survives a Glyphs round trip (`test_propagate_anchors_survives_a_glyphs_round_trip`) and is stated once when written again.
